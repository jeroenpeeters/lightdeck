Tray chip, notice

Chip: 48 high. Dashed = what the look itself uses. Amber = on now. Pressing a chip other than the look's own makes the look Varied. All puts the look back as stored.
Home Assistant is simulated: no real lamp is touched.
Notices lie over the keys at the bottom of the middle area, never push them, and fade after 6 s. Only one at a time.

Blackout and laser

Outlined in red when idle. When active it is filled red and a 3 px red frame runs round the whole screen.
safe
arming: 1 s fill
armed: hazard frame
Laser looks are disabled until it is armed. No sequence, effect or look of another group can open it. Blackout closes and disarms it.
