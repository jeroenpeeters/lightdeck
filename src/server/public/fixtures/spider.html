Swing limit per bar

swing limit zone tilt now

No bar tilts beyond its limit, whatever sets it: the sliders below, a scene or a movement. A tilt set by hand is cut off there; a movement is fitted into the limits and swings all the way from one to the other. The limit is kept in the show file.

Zone

How far the movement reaches. The movement is laid inside this window: its 0 is where a bar starts and its 100 where it stops, so a narrow window is a small swing. It takes the place of the swing limit while it is on. A tilt set by hand is not held to it. Store it on the deck, in Program, as a Zone scene to press it later.
