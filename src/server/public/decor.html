Pipes
Order and direction
    As mounted

    Link the pipes and one effect runs through them one after the other, in the order set here, as if they were a single pipe: it enters the first pipe and leaves the last. The effect, the palette and the pace are then one for all of them, on the live page too. Switching a pipe on or off and its brightness stay per pipe, and the effect goes on through a pipe that is off.

    Reverse turns a pipe the other way round, for how you hang it: the effect then enters it at its last LED. It works linked or apart. Both are kept in decor.json, so they are still there after a restart.

    Only the pipes that lightdeck draws are here. A pipe left to the effects of WLED (draw: wled in the rig file) cannot be part of a chain.
